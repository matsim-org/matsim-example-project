Per-Scenario Interactive Maps

Click a scenario name to open its interactive map. Each map has four togglable layers:

Traffic Volume

Link width and color by daily vehicle count. Blue (low) → Red (high).

Congestion (V/C)

Volume-to-Capacity ratio. Green (free-flow) → Red (over capacity).

Speed vs Free-Flow

Actual speed as percentage of free-flow. Green (fast) → Red (slow).

AM Peak Congestion

V/C ratio during 07:00–09:00 AM peak only.
